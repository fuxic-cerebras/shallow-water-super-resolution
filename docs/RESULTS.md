## Limitations

- Error grows strongly with lead time because the coarse and fine members of each pair are **independent** integrations (D002) and progressively decorrelate. Beyond roughly 30 hours the coarse state carries little information about the specific fine realization, so error there is not recoverable by any method.
- Consequently a single aggregate number mixes a well-posed regime with a partly unpredictable one. The lead-time table above is the honest reading; the aggregate is a summary of a mixture.
- No data augmentation is used: reflections and the transpose are not symmetries of a rotating beta-plane (D018), so the effective training set is the trajectory count times the number of decorrelated states per trajectory.
- Results are for the tested Gaussian initial-condition family on a closed rotating basin. No claim of physical generalization beyond it is made.
